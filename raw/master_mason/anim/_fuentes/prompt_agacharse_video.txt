EL PROMPT
---------

Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) filling the
whole frame, no shadows, no ground, no floor line, no props, no text, no logos,
no on-screen interface or player controls.

Digital illustration animation of the ancient pilgrim from the first frame (long
white beard, rounded slate-blue hood, patchwork brown geometric robe), seen in
side profile facing right. He holds completely still in his hunched standing
pose for one second. Then, in one single smooth and continuous motion that takes
about one second, he lowers himself into a deep squat: his knees bend, his bare
feet stay planted flat on the same spot without moving, and his hands slide down
his thighs to rest on his knees. He slows down gently as he reaches the bottom
and comes to rest exactly in the pose of the last frame, softly, with no bounce,
no rebound, no dipping below the final pose and no swaying or settling movements
afterwards. Then he holds that squatting pose with his hands on his knees,
completely still apart from a very subtle breath, until the end of the video. He
does not stand up again.

His size never changes and his position on screen does not move. The character
design, colours and textures of the reference frames are strictly maintained
throughout: same low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted style, soft matte texture,
crisp frames with no motion blur, no flicker. Sound: only the soft rustle of the
robe, no music, no voice.
