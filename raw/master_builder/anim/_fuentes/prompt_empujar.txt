PROMPT PARA EMPUJAR UN PESO: ENTRADA, CICLO Y SALIDA EN UN SOLO VIDEO
(Google Flow / Veo 3.1, "Frames to Video", 2026-10-11)
=====================================================================
Por que: el guion v4 pide empujar (acciones_del_guion.md, n.o 4: piedras, el
pendulo, troncos, sillares, la carretilla; colocar sillar = empujar hasta la
marca). Un solo video que empieza y acaba en nuestro reposo: la entrada (se
apoya en el peso), el ciclo (empuja andando) y la salida (lo suelta y se queda
quieto).

Sin baston en el video: el baston guardado lo pinta el juego encima
(seguir.py), como en andar, correr, agacharse o el giro agachado. Asi la misma
animacion vale con el baston a la espalda y sin el.

En Flow, modo de fotogramas a video:
  - fotograma inicial: referencia_empujar_inicio.png  (el reposo del juego,
    peregrino_respirando c_01, mirando a la derecha, con un sillar delante a medio
    paso: 125 px de la punta del pie a la cara del sillar)
  - fotograma final:   referencia_empujar_fin.png     (lo mismo 439 px mas a la
    derecha: lo que lo ha empujado)
  (las dos aqui en _fuentes/ y copiadas en Descargas; 1920x1080, croma #24D732,
  sprite de master 1:1, pies en y=1040; las hace referencias_empujar.py, aqui
  al lado)
  El sillar es de relleno: 389x345 (0,55 del alto del peregrino, algo por encima
  de la cintura), piedra clara low-poly para separarlo bien de la tunica. Se
  quita al montar y el juego pone sus objetos. Las manos van a la altura de su
  filo: si el objeto del juego va a ser mucho mas alto o mas bajo, rehacer la
  referencia.
  439 px en ~4 s de empuje = ~110 px/s de master, unas 3,5 veces menos que
  andar (384).
  - 16:9, Veo 3.1 - Quality, 1080p, 8 s, con audio. Sacar 3-4 tomas.
  El audio, sin ruido de piedra: asi vale para empujar cualquier cosa y el roce
  lo pone cada objeto.

Que mirar en cada toma, por orden:
  1. de perfil todo el rato, mirando a la derecha: no se vuelve hacia la camara
     ni ensena la espalda
  2. camara quieta: el y el sillar cruzan el cuadro (nada de paneo ni zoom)
  3. el sillar solo se mueve mientras el empuja, deslizandose recto: sin volcar,
     girar, saltar ni cambiar de forma o tamano; se para cuando el para
  4. las manos apoyadas en la cara del sillar, sin atravesarla
  5. pasos cortos y pesados con el cuerpo inclinado; el pie apoyado no patina
  6. a velocidad real, sin camara lenta: ~1,5 s para apoyarse, ~4 s empujando,
     ~1,5 s para soltarlo y quedarse quieto
  7. la capucha como la del primer fotograma (sin pico de sombrero de mago) y
     la cara casi tapada por su sombra
  8. nada encima (controles, marcas de agua, texto): si sale, pedir otra toma
     pronto
Si el ciclo no cierra en bucle, despues se pide otro video con inicio = fin = un
fotograma del empuje, como se hizo con el andar con el baston.

EL PROMPT
---------

Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. The camera does not follow the action: the pilgrim and the
stone block move across the frame from left to right. Flat pure chroma-key
green background (#24D732) filling the whole frame, no shadows, no cast shadow
on the ground, no floor line, no other props, no text, no logos, no on-screen
interface or player controls. Everything stays full-body in frame, always the
same size.

2D side view. The old hooded pilgrim from the first frame stays in strict side
profile facing RIGHT for the whole video: he never turns his body or his head
towards the camera and never shows his back. His head is covered by the soft
grey cloth hood of his cloak, exactly the shape of the first frame: part of the
cloak, not a hat, no tall point, no cone; its deep shadow hides most of his
face. Long white beard, long brown faceted patchwork cloak in dark maroon,
brick red, ochre, warm brown and muted grey, thin dark legs and bare feet. His
hands are bare and empty.

In front of him, on the floor, stands the large block of pale dressed stone
from the first frame, a mason's ashlar, a little higher than his waist. It is
enormously heavy: it only moves while he pushes it with all his weight, sliding
slowly and evenly along the floor. It never tips, rotates, jumps or bounces,
never slides on its own, and keeps exactly its shape, size and colour.

Action, one continuous take, at natural real-time speed, no slow motion: the
weight shows in his posture and his short steps, not in slowing down the video.
- 0 to 1 s: he stands still exactly as in the first frame.
- 1 to 2.5 s: he reaches forward and places both hands flat on the near face of
  the block, just below its top edge, sets one foot back and leans his whole
  weight into it: his body tilted forward about 45 degrees, arms bent, head
  lowered between his shoulders, front knee bent, back leg stretched out behind
  him on the ball of the foot.
- 2.5 to 6.5 s: he pushes. The block starts to slide slowly to the right and he
  follows it with short, slow, heavy steps, about one step per second, digging
  his bare feet into the floor; his hands stay pressed on the stone and his
  body stays leaned into it at the same angle. Each planted foot stays fixed on
  the floor until it lifts: no sliding feet. His hands never sink into the
  stone. The block moves exactly as fast as he walks.
- 6.5 to 8 s: he stops pushing and the block stops at once. He pushes himself
  upright off the stone, takes his hands off it, brings his back foot forward
  next to the other one and stands still exactly as in the last frame, half a
  step behind the block, which now rests further to the right.

Same low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted style, colours and lighting as the first
frame for both the pilgrim and the stone, crisp frames with no motion blur,
soft matte texture, no flicker. Sound: only the old man's strained breathing
and low grunts of effort, his bare footsteps and the rustle of the heavy cloth;
the stone itself makes no sound; no music, no words.
