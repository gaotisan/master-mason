EL PROMPT
---------
2D side-view game animation for a side-scrolling game. The camera follows the
character sideways at exactly his walking speed, so he stays in the centre of
the frame the whole time, always the same size; there is no visible background
movement because the background is a flat pure chroma-key green (#24D732)
everywhere: no ground, no floor line, no shadows, no scenery, no text, no
on-screen interface.

Character: exactly the character in the first frame, unchanged: an old master
builder, wizard-like, tall pointed grey hood, long white beard, full-length
cloak of flat geometric triangular facets in dark maroon, brick red, ochre,
warm brown and muted grey that keep exactly the same pattern for the whole
video, thin dark legs, bare feet. Low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted look, no
outlines. Strict side profile facing RIGHT the whole video.

He holds a long wooden walking staff upright in his front hand, at chest
height, its top an open claw of curled root fingers. The claw is EMPTY: no orb,
no ball, no gem, no glow, no light.

Action, one continuous take:
- 0 to 1 s: he stands completely still, exactly as in the first frame.
- 1 to 6.5 s: he starts walking forward and walks at a slow, steady, even pace,
  like a tired old pilgrim: about one step every 0.8 seconds, all steps the same
  length and the same rhythm. He uses the staff as a walking stick in a regular
  rhythm: on EVERY step of his front foot he lifts the staff cleanly off the
  floor, swings it a little forward and plants its tip firmly on the floor
  ahead of him at the same moment that foot lands; while the tip is on the floor
  it stays fixed at that spot and he walks past it. The staff tip never slides
  or drags along the floor.
- 6.5 to 7.5 s: he slows down over his last step, plants the staff and stops.
- 7.5 to 8 s: he stands completely still, exactly as in the last frame.

Crisp frames, no motion blur, no flicker. Sound: only soft bare footsteps, the
light knock of the staff on the floor at each plant and the rustle of the
cloak; no music, no voice.
