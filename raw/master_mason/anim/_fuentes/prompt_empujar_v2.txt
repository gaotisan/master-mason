PROMPT PARA EMPUJAR v2: MANOS ENTERAS EN VERTICAL, COMO UNA PUERTA, A MEDIA ALTURA
(Google Flow / Veo 3.1, "Frames to Video", 2026-10-11)
==================================================================================
Por que: el usuario quiere las manos enteras, planas y en vertical, "como si
empujaras una puerta", y no apoyadas en el filo de arriba del sillar
(prompt_empujar.txt, la v1). Empujando a media altura de un bloque alto, la
animacion es mas flexible: las manos van a ~0,45 del alto del peregrino (la
cadera), por debajo del filo del sillar de la v1 (0,55), asi que vale para esa
piedra y para cualquier cosa mas alta.

Lo demas como la v1: un solo video que empieza y acaba en nuestro reposo, con
la entrada (se apoya), el ciclo (empuja andando) y la salida (lo suelta). Sin
baston en el video (el guardado lo pinta el juego encima, seguir.py).

En Flow, modo de fotogramas a video:
  - fotograma inicial: referencia_empujar_v2_inicio.png  (el reposo del juego,
    magnus_respirando c_01, mirando a la derecha, con un sillar alto delante a
    un brazo: 219 px de la punta del pie a la cara del sillar)
  - fotograma final:   referencia_empujar_v2_fin.png     (lo mismo 439 px mas a
    la derecha: lo que lo ha empujado)
  (las dos aqui en _fuentes/ y copiadas en Descargas; 1920x1080, croma #24D732,
  sprite de master 1:1, pies en y=1040; las hace "referencias_empujar.py v2",
  aqui al lado)
  El sillar es de relleno: 439x564 (0,9 del alto del peregrino: le llega a la
  cara), con la cara de empuje recta. Se quita al montar y el juego pone sus
  objetos.
  439 px en ~4 s de empuje = ~110 px/s de master, unas 3,5 veces menos que
  andar (384).
  - 16:9, Veo 3.1 - Quality, 1080p, 8 s, con audio. Sacar 3-4 tomas.
  El audio, sin ruido de piedra: asi vale para empujar cualquier cosa y el roce
  lo pone cada objeto.

Que mirar en cada toma, por orden:
  1. las manos enteras, planas y en vertical (dedos hacia arriba) contra la cara
     del sillar, A MEDIA ALTURA (a la altura de la cadera), y ahi todo el rato:
     si suben al pecho o a los hombros, con un objeto bajo asomarian por encima;
     nunca agarra el filo ni las esquinas
  2. de perfil todo el rato, mirando a la derecha: no se vuelve hacia la camara
     ni ensena la espalda
  3. camara quieta: el y el sillar cruzan el cuadro (nada de paneo ni zoom)
  4. el sillar solo se mueve mientras el empuja, deslizandose recto: sin volcar,
     girar, saltar ni cambiar de forma o tamano; se para cuando el para
  5. las manos sin atravesar la piedra
  6. pasos cortos y pesados con el cuerpo inclinado; el pie apoyado no patina
  7. a velocidad real, sin camara lenta: ~1,5 s para apoyarse, ~4 s empujando,
     ~1,5 s para soltarlo y quedarse quieto
  8. la capucha como la del primer fotograma (sin pico de sombrero de mago) y
     la cara casi tapada por su sombra
  9. nada encima (controles, marcas de agua, texto): si sale, pedir otra toma
     pronto
Si el ciclo no cierra en bucle, despues se pide otro video con inicio = fin = un
fotograma del empuje, como se hizo con el andar con el baston.

EL PROMPT
---------

Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. The camera does not follow the action: the pilgrim and the
stone block move across the frame from left to right. Flat pure chroma-key
green background (#24D732) filling the whole frame, no shadows, no cast shadow
on the ground, no floor line, no other props, no text, no logos, no on-screen
interface or player controls. Everything stays full-body in frame, always the
same size.

2D side view. The old hooded pilgrim from the first frame stays in strict side
profile facing RIGHT for the whole video: he never turns his body or his head
towards the camera and never shows his back. His head is covered by the soft
grey cloth hood of his cloak, exactly the shape of the first frame: part of the
cloak, not a hat, no tall point, no cone; its deep shadow hides most of his
face. Long white beard, long brown faceted patchwork cloak in dark maroon,
brick red, ochre, warm brown and muted grey, thin dark legs and bare feet. His
hands are bare and empty.

In front of him, on the floor, stands the tall block of pale dressed stone from
the first frame, a mason's ashlar as tall as his shoulders, with a flat
vertical face towards him. It is enormously heavy: it only moves while he
pushes it with all his weight, sliding slowly and evenly along the floor. It
never tips, rotates, jumps or bounces, never slides on its own, and keeps
exactly its shape, size and colour.

How he pushes: exactly like pushing open a heavy door. Both hands are pressed
flat and upright against the vertical face of the stone, LOW on the block, at
about half its height, level with his hips, not at chest or shoulder height:
open palms with the fingers pointing straight up, the whole hand, from the heel
of the palm to the fingertips, flat on the stone, one hand beside the other.
His arms reach forward, elbows only slightly bent. He never grips the top edge
or the corners of the block: only his flat open palms touch the stone, and they
stay at that same height the whole time.

Action, one continuous take, at natural real-time speed, no slow motion: the
weight shows in his posture and his short steps, not in slowing down the video.
- 0 to 1 s: he stands still exactly as in the first frame.
- 1 to 2.5 s: he steps forward with one foot, leans in and presses both hands
  flat and upright against the face of the block at hip height, then leans his
  whole weight into it: his body tilted forward about 45 degrees, head lowered
  between his shoulders, front knee bent, back leg stretched out behind him on
  the ball of the foot.
- 2.5 to 6.5 s: he pushes. The block starts to slide slowly to the right and he
  follows it with short, slow, heavy steps, about one step per second, digging
  his bare feet into the floor; his hands stay flat and upright on the stone at
  hip height and his body stays leaned into it at the same angle. Each planted
  foot stays fixed on the floor until it lifts: no sliding feet. His hands
  never sink into the stone. The block moves exactly as fast as he walks.
- 6.5 to 8 s: he stops pushing and the block stops at once. He pushes himself
  upright off the stone, takes his hands off it, brings his back foot forward
  next to the other one and stands still exactly as in the last frame, an arm's
  length behind the block, which now rests further to the right.

Same low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted style, colours and lighting as the first
frame for both the pilgrim and the stone, crisp frames with no motion blur,
soft matte texture, no flicker. Sound: only the old man's strained breathing
and low grunts of effort, his bare footsteps and the rustle of the heavy cloth;
the stone itself makes no sound; no music, no words.
