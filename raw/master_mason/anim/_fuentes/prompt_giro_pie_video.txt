PROMPT PARA EL VIDEO DEL GIRO DE PIE (Google Flow / Veo, "Frames to Video")
=========================================================================
El giro de pie (magnus_giro) son 5 vistas a 45 grados de una hoja de IA: se ve
brusco al lado del resto, que sale de video. Con un video del giro hay angulos
de sobra para elegir y queda tan fluido como andar o correr.

En Flow, modo de fotogramas a video:
  - fotograma inicial: referencia_giro_pie_video_inicio.png  (el reposo del juego,
    magnus_respirando c_08, mirando a la derecha, a su tamano de master: 628 px de
    alto en 1920x1080, pies en la fila 880)
  - fotograma final:   referencia_giro_pie_video_fin.png     (el mismo, espejado)
  (las dos estan aqui en _fuentes/ y copiadas en Descargas)
  - 16:9, 8 s, el modelo de mas calidad. Sacar 2-4 tomas y quedarse con la buena.

Que mirar en cada toma, por orden (lo que no tiene arreglo despues):
  1. la camara quieta: nada de zoom ni de paneo
  2. que no cambie de tamano ni de postura (que no se encorve mas ni se estire), y
     que la cabeza vaya siempre a la misma altura
  3. que gire hacia la camara y nunca ensene la espalda
  4. que los pies pisen siempre en la misma linea y no se desplace por la pantalla
  5. que no haya nada encima (controles de reproductor, marcas de agua, texto)
  6. que el giro vaya a velocidad constante, sin parones
Si ensena la espalda, otra toma; si sigue, probar sin fotograma final.

EL PROMPT
---------

Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) filling the
whole frame, no shadows, no ground, no floor line, no props, no text, no logos,
no on-screen interface or player controls.

The old hooded wizard from the first frame stands in exactly the same relaxed
standing pose the whole time: slightly stooped, arms hanging relaxed at his
sides, the long faceted cloak down to his ankles, thin dark legs and bare feet
visible below it. His posture, his height and his size never change, and his
head stays at the same height.

He holds still for one second. Then he slowly turns around on the spot to face
the other way, rotating towards the camera: from his side profile facing right,
through a three-quarter view, to facing the camera directly, then on through a
three-quarter view to his side profile facing left. The turn is smooth, slow and
even, at a constant speed, taking about five seconds, with small, light steps of
his bare feet in place; his feet stay on the same line and his position on
screen does not move. He never turns his back to the camera. Then he holds still
for one second in the final pose, identical to the last frame.

Same low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted style, same colours and lighting as the
first frame throughout, soft matte texture, no flicker. Sound: only the soft
rustle of the cloak and his bare feet on the ground, no music, no voice.
