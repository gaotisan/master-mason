PROMPT PARA EL GIRO CON EL BASTON EN LA MANO (Google Flow, Frames to Video)
==========================================================================
Rehecho el 2026-10-07 con lo aprendido en el giro de pie sin baston
(prompt_giro_pie_rapido.txt) y en sacar/guardar:
  - a la velocidad del juego (~1,5 s) y con DOS pasos claros: un giro pedido
    lento con pasitos no se puede acelerar (se ve "a saltitos" / pies que
    se arrastran);
  - por la ESPALDA, como el giro sin baston del juego, que asi se ven igual;
  - referencias en 1080p: tienen aire encima de la cabeza y el baston no se
    sale por arriba si lo levanta.

Basta MEDIA vuelta (180 grados): de perfil derecho a perfil izquierdo. La
vuelta hacia el otro lado es la misma espejada (flip_h).

En Flow, modo fotogramas a video:
  inicio = baston_ref_en_mano_1080.png            (reposo con el baston, mirando a la derecha)
  fin    = baston_ref_en_mano_izquierda_1080.png  (el mismo, espejado)
  (las dos en _fuentes/ y en Descargas)
  16:9, 1080p, 8 s, con audio. Sacar 2-4 tomas.

Que mirar en cada toma, por orden:
  1. que el giro dure poco (1-1,5 s), seguido, sin pararse a medias
  2. los pies: dos pasos claros que pivotan, no muchos pasitos; en el sitio
  3. el baston: sigue en la mano, la punta vuelve al suelo al acabar, no se
     sale del video ni se cruza delante de la cara; garra vacia
  4. camara quieta, mismo tamano, cabeza a la misma altura
  5. la tunica no cambia de dibujo; nada encima (controles, marcas)
Si pasa por delante en vez de por la espalda, vale igual si el resto esta bien.

EL PROMPT
---------
Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) filling the
whole frame, no shadows, no ground, no floor line, no props, no text, no logos,
no on-screen interface or player controls.

Character: exactly the character in the first frame, unchanged: an old master
builder, wizard-like, tall pointed grey hood, long white beard, full-length
cloak of flat geometric triangular facets in dark maroon, brick red, ochre,
warm brown and muted grey that keep exactly the same pattern for the whole
video, thin dark legs, bare feet. Low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted look, no
outlines. His posture, his height and his size never change, and his head
stays at the same height.

He holds a long wooden walking staff upright in his front hand at chest height,
its tip on the floor; at the top the staff opens into an open claw of curled
root fingers, EMPTY: no orb, no ball, no gem, no glow, no light. He keeps the
staff in the same hand the whole time, and the whole staff stays inside the
frame at all times.

He stands completely still for two seconds. Then, in one single quick and
natural movement, he turns around to face the other way, turning his BACK to
the camera as he turns, the way an old man turns back when he hears someone
call him from behind: the whole turn takes only about one and a half seconds,
a pivot with just two short, clear, natural steps of his bare feet, from his
side profile facing right, through a view from behind, to his side profile
facing left, without stopping halfway and without any extra small steps. The
staff turns with him: he lifts its tip just off the floor, carries it round
close to his body and plants it again on the floor in front of him as the turn
ends. His feet stay on the same line and his position on screen does not move.
Then he stands completely still in the final pose, identical to the last frame,
until the end of the video.

Crisp frames, no motion blur, no flicker. Sound: the soft rustle of the cloak,
two light bare footsteps and a dry wooden knock when the staff is planted
again. No music, no voice.

SI SALE MAL
-----------
- Lento o con pasitos: otra toma; no acelerar.
- El baston cambia de mano o desaparece tras el cuerpo mucho rato: otra toma,
  repetir "he keeps the staff in the same hand the whole time".
- Pinta bola o brillo en la garra: repetir "the claw is EMPTY, no orb".
