EL PROMPT
---------
2D side-view game animation of a seamless walk cycle for a side-scrolling
game. The camera follows the character sideways at exactly his walking speed,
so he stays in the centre of the frame the whole time, always the same size.
Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) everywhere: no ground, no floor
line, no shadows, no scenery, no text, no on-screen interface.

Character: exactly the character in the first frame, unchanged for the whole
video: an old master builder, wizard-like, grey pointed hood exactly as in the
first frame, long white beard, full-length cloak of flat geometric triangular
facets that keep exactly the same pattern, the same sleeves and the same
arms as in the first frame, thin dark legs, bare feet. Low-poly flat-shaded
hand-painted look, no outlines. Strict side profile facing RIGHT.

He holds the long wooden walking staff in his front hand exactly as in the
first frame; its top is an open claw of curled root fingers, EMPTY: no orb, no
ball, no gem, no glow, no light. His other hand hangs relaxed at his side.

Action: from the very first frame to the very last frame he keeps walking
forward without stopping, at the same slow, steady, even pace he already has in
the first frame, every step the same length and the same rhythm, using the
staff as a walking stick in a regular rhythm: he lifts it, swings it a little
forward and plants its tip on the floor ahead of him, always at the same moment
of his stride; the tip never slides on the floor. He does not slow down, stop
or pause at any point: the video ends while he is still walking, at exactly the
same moment of the stride and in exactly the same pose as the first frame, so
the video loops seamlessly.

Crisp frames, no motion blur, no flicker. Sound: soft bare footsteps, the light
knock of the staff at each plant and the rustle of the cloak; no music, no
voice.
