PROMPT PARA EL VIDEO DEL GIRO AGACHADO (Google Flow / Veo, "Frames to Video")
===========================================================================
Para que el giro agachado sea mas fluido que las 5 vistas de la hoja de Gemini.

En Flow, modo de fotogramas a video:
  - fotograma inicial: referencia_giro_video_inicio.png  (agachado del juego, mirando
    a la derecha, a su tamano de master: 486 px de alto en 1920x1080, pies en la fila 880)
  - fotograma final:   referencia_giro_video_fin.png     (el mismo, espejado)
  (las dos estan aqui en _fuentes/ y copiadas en Descargas)
  - 16:9, 8 s, el modelo de mas calidad. Sacar 2-4 tomas y quedarse con la buena.

Que mirar en cada toma, por orden (lo que no tiene arreglo despues):
  1. la camara quieta: nada de zoom ni de paneo (el video del andar agachado traia
     zoom y hubo que corregirlo fotograma a fotograma)
  2. que no se levante ni cambie de tamano, y que la cabeza vaya siempre a la misma altura
  3. que gire hacia la camara y nunca ensene la espalda
  4. que no haya nada encima (controles de reproductor, marcas de agua, texto)
  5. que el giro vaya a velocidad constante, sin parones
Si ensena la espalda o se levanta, otra toma; si sigue, probar sin fotograma final.
El audio: si el roce de la ropa sale limpio y sin musica, sirve para el giro.

EL PROMPT
---------

Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all: no pan, no zoom, no dolly,
no tilt, no cuts. Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) filling the
whole frame, no shadows, no ground, no floor line, no props, no text, no logos,
no on-screen interface or player controls.

The old hooded wizard from the first frame stays crouched in exactly the same
low, hunched pose the whole time: back rounded, head forward, both hands resting
on his knees, the heavy faceted cloak draping down to the ground and covering
his legs, only the tips of his bare toes showing. He never stands up, never
rises, his head stays at the same height and the size of the character never
changes.

He holds still for one second. Then, staying crouched, he slowly turns around on
the spot to face the other way, rotating towards the camera: from his side
profile facing right, through a three-quarter view, to facing the camera
directly, then on through a three-quarter view to his side profile facing left.
The turn is smooth, slow and even, at a constant speed, taking about five
seconds, pivoting in place with small shuffling steps of his feet hidden under
the cloak; his position on screen does not move. He never turns his back to the
camera. Then he holds still for one second in the final pose, identical to the
last frame.

Same low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted style, same colours and lighting as the
first frame throughout, soft matte texture, no flicker. Sound: only the soft
rustle of the heavy cloth as he turns, no music, no voice.
