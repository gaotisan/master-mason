ARRANQUE
--------
2D side-view game animation for a side-scrolling game. The camera follows the
character sideways so he always stays in the centre of the frame, always the
same size. Flat pure chroma-key green background (#24D732) everywhere: no
ground, no floor line, no shadows, no scenery, no text, no on-screen interface.

Character: exactly the character in the first frame, unchanged for the whole
video: an old master builder, wizard-like, grey hood exactly as in the first
frame, long white beard, full-length cloak of flat geometric triangular facets
that keep exactly the same pattern, thin dark legs, bare feet. Low-poly
flat-shaded hand-painted look, no outlines. Strict side profile facing RIGHT.
He holds a long wooden walking staff in his front hand; its top is an open claw
of curled root fingers, EMPTY: no orb, no ball, no gem, no glow, no light.

Action: he stands completely still for about one second, exactly as in the
first frame. Then he starts walking forward calmly, like a tired old pilgrim,
and within about one and a half seconds he is walking at his slow, steady
pace, using the staff as a walking stick in rhythm with his steps (he lifts
it, swings it a little forward and plants its tip on the floor; it never
slides). He keeps walking at that same steady pace, without slowing down,
until the very last frame, which is exactly the pose of the last frame image,
still in the middle of his stride.

Crisp frames, no motion blur, no flicker. Sound: soft bare footsteps, the light
knock of the staff and the rustle of the cloak; no music, no voice.
