===================================================
Primer fotograma: primer_fotograma_recoger_baston.png (1280x720). Es

EL PROMPT
---------

2D side-view game animation. Static locked camera, flat pure chroma-key green
background (#24D732) for the whole video. No ground, no floor texture, no
shadows, no cast shadow under the feet, no horizon, no scenery, no text.
Orthographic feel, camera at chest height, no pan, no zoom, no cuts. The
character is full-body in frame at all times, nothing cropped.

Character: exactly the character in the first frame, unchanged. An old master
builder, wizard-like. Tall pointed grey-blue hood covering the head, long white
beard to mid-chest. Full-length heavy cloak to the ankles with a pointed hem,
built from flat geometric triangular facets in dark maroon, brick red, ochre,
warm brown and muted grey. Thin dark grey legs and bare skin-toned feet.
Low-poly flat-shaded hand-painted look, soft matte texture, no outlines, no rim
light. He stays in strict side profile facing RIGHT for the whole video: he
never turns toward the camera and never shows his back.

Prop: a long wooden walking staff lies on the floor ahead of him, to the right,
lengthwise along his path, fully visible. Straight, slightly gnarled, dark
weathered wood, a small knot at the top end, roughly as long as he is tall.
Same low-poly faceted matte style as the character.

Action, one continuous take:
- 0 to 0.5 s: he stands still in the exact pose of the first frame.
- 0.5 to 3 s: he walks slowly to the right, three calm steps of an old man,
  arms relaxed with a small natural swing, one swing per stride.
- 3 to 3.5 s: he stops where the staff begins, feet together, a brief pause.
- 3.5 to 5 s: he bends down slowly and carefully, knees bending, back curving,
  the near hand reaching down and grabbing the staff near its middle.
- 5 to 6 s: he straightens up with the staff in his hand.
- 6 to 7.5 s: he lifts it and slides it diagonally behind his back, tucking it
  under the hood and the collar of the cloak so it stays there by itself: the
  top end sticks out above and behind his head, the bottom end reaches the
  back of his calves.
- 7.5 to 8 s: he lets go and stands still in the same neutral pose as the
  first frame, arms relaxed at his sides, the staff resting diagonally across
  his back.

Motion: slow, weighty, deliberate, an old man careful with his back. The hood
and beard stay on and unchanged. The cloak moves only a little.

Audio: only soft footsteps of bare feet, cloth rustling, a dry wooden knock
when he picks up the staff, one faint effort breath when he bends down. No
music, no voice, no ambience.

- La camara le sigue (como en los videos de andar): da igual para sacar
