PROMPT (hoja completa)
----------------------
Character turnaround sheet of the exact same character shown in the reference
images: an old hooded pilgrim with a pointed grey hood, a long white beard, and
a long faceted patchwork cloak of muted rust, ochre, plum and slate-grey
geometric patches, thin dark legs and bare feet. Keep his design, proportions,
colours and painterly flat faceted style exactly as in reference image 1. Do not
add or remove anything: no staff, no belt, no bag, no new clothing.

Five full-body views in one row, left to right, all facing right:
1. right side profile
2. three-quarter front: mostly side view, turned slightly toward the viewer
   (about 35 degrees from the profile), we see a little of his chest, the beard
   and both feet
3. full front view, facing the viewer
4. three-quarter back: mostly side view, turned slightly away from the viewer
   (about 35 degrees from the profile), we see part of his back and the hood
   from behind, the face is hidden
5. full back view: the hood covers his head completely, no face and no beard
   visible, the patchwork cloak seen from behind

Same neutral standing pose in all five views: standing straight, arms relaxed at
his sides, hands visible below the sleeves, feet together.

Camera: slightly above eye level, about 12 degrees, looking down a little at
him, so the top of the hood and the feet are seen slightly from above. Long
telephoto lens, no perspective distortion, the same camera height and distance
for every view.

All five figures the same height, feet on the same horizontal ground line,
evenly spaced, not overlapping. Flat uniform pure chroma green background
(#00FF00), no floor, no cast shadows, no text, no labels, no arrows.
